Escarpment, ocean and everything in between

Wollongong sits between rainforest-covered cliffs and the Pacific Ocean. In one day you can stand on a lookout, walk out over the sea, visit two of the Southern Hemisphere's largest temples and watch a blowhole erupt. Here are our favourite places in the Illawarra and along the South Coast.

Featured

Broker's Nose

Broker's Nose is a sandstone headland on the Illawarra Escarpment above Corrimal and Bulli. It is a launch spot for hang gliders, and it has one of the best views of Wollongong: the city, the beaches and the coastline running south toward Port Kembla.

You reach the top by a bushwalk from Mount Ousley Road. Go early in the morning or near sunset for the best light.

Lookouts and coastal drives

Grand Pacific Drive

Sea Cliff Bridge Lookout

Clifton · about 30 min north of Wollongong

The Sea Cliff Bridge curves out over the ocean between Coalcliff and Clifton. Walk the footpath on the ocean side, then take in the full curve of the bridge and the cliffs from the lookout.

Stanwell Park

Bald Hill Lookout

Stanwell Park · start of the Grand Pacific Drive

This is the classic view down the northern Illawarra coast. Hang gliders and paragliders launch from the hill. Aviation pioneer Lawrence Hargrave tested his box kites on the beach below.

Escarpment

Mount Keira Lookout

Mount Keira · 15 min from the CBD

You can drive almost to the top. From there you see the whole city from Thirroul down to Port Kembla and Lake Illawarra. There is a café at the summit, and the night views are lovely.

Shoalhaven

Cambewarra Mountain Lookout

Near Kangaroo Valley · about 1 hr 15 min south

A mountain-top lookout over the Shoalhaven River, the farmland of the Shoalhaven and, on clear days, the coast toward Jervis Bay. The kiosk is a good place to stop for scones and a cuppa.

Wollongong CBD

Walk around Belmore Basin past the fishing boats to Flagstaff Hill and the city's two lighthouses. Then grab fish and chips and watch the surf at North Beach.

Temples

Berkeley

Nan Tien Temple

Berkeley · 15 min south of the CBD

The largest Buddhist temple in the Southern Hemisphere. Wander the shrines, the gardens and the eight-storey pagoda. Lunch at the vegetarian dining hall, or have tea at the teahouse.

Official site → Open in Maps →
Helensburgh

Sri Venkateswara Temple

Helensburgh · about 30 min north

A traditional South Indian Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Venkateswara (Balaji). It sits in quiet bushland near the Royal National Park. Visitors are welcome to see the temple towers and take part in daily worship.

Kiama and further south

Kiama

Kiama Blowhole

Blowhole Point · about 40 min south

When the swell comes from the south-east, the ocean shoots up through the rock and sprays high into the air. The Kiama Lighthouse stands right next to it, and the harbour is a short walk away.

Open in Maps →
Kiama

Little Blowhole

Kiama Heights · 5 min from the main blowhole

Smaller, but often more active than its big brother, and the crowds are thinner. There is a short walk from the car park, and the rugged basalt coastline makes great photos.

Rainforest

Illawarra Fly Treetop Walk

Knights Hill · about 50 min south-west

Walk through the rainforest canopy on high steel walkways and climb the lookout tower. You can see all the way across the escarpment to the coast.
